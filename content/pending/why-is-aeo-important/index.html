Why is AEO important?

AEO matters because the answer is replacing the click: when a system composes a reply and names three sources, being one of those three is the entire visible outcome, and everyone else is absent rather than ranked tenth. It is a change in what winning looks like, not a change in how hard the work is.

By Allan Oliveira · Reviewed 26 September 2026

The honest case for AEO is narrower than the marketing case, and it is stronger for being narrower. Here it is without the urgency.

Under a list of linksUnder a composed answer
Ten results, several get attentionOne answer, two or three sources named
Position two still earns clicksNot cited means not present
Your snippet is your wordsYour words are paraphrased by someone else
A console reports your positionNobody reports anything; you observe it or you do not know

The row that matters most is the last one

Search gave you a console. Answer engines give you nothing: no impressions, no positions, no citation count. A whole channel became invisible to measurement at the same moment it became consequential. That is the actual reason this discipline needs a name, and it is why the measurement problem comes before the optimization problem.

The part usually oversold

Nobody serious knows how much traffic has moved, and the published figures vary by an order of magnitude depending on who is selling. Treat anyone with a precise percentage as someone with a product. What can be observed directly, by anyone, is far more modest and far more useful: ask the questions your customers ask, and see who gets named.

What the observation usually shows

In the runs published in this site’s own log, engines attributed a source in most answers. They cite readily. The uncomfortable finding is who they cite: in sixteen observations of a separate brand-level run, an engine named some source in fourteen of them and the brand being measured in none. The market is not "AI does not cite anyone". It is that it cites somebody else, which is a solvable problem and a measurable one.

Who this is not urgent for

If the queries your customers type still return an ordinary page of links, this is a hedge rather than a channel, and the technical foundation you would build anyway is most of the work. The comparison with SEO covers where the two diverge. Nobody can tell you which case you are in without running your queries.

Why the urgency is usually overstated

The sales pitch for this discipline runs on a cliff: act now or disappear. The observable reality is slower and more boring. Plenty of commercial queries still return an ordinary page of links with no composed answer above it, and on those queries nothing has changed. The transition is real and uneven, and the only way to know where your market sits is to run your own queries and look.

What it means for a small site

Counterintuitively, the shift is not uniformly bad for small publishers. A ranked list rewards accumulated authority, and ten positions go to the same large domains for years. A composed answer needs a passage that states the thing clearly, and a small site that answers a narrow question precisely can be the cheapest source available on it. In the runs recorded here, a short-form video with no distribution behind it was cited by a Google AI Overview while a network of eight coordinated domains was not.

The one thing worth doing this month

Write down the ten questions your customers actually ask, in their words, and run them across three engines while logged out. Record what comes back, including who else gets named. That single afternoon tells you more about whether this matters for your business than any forecast, and it is the baseline everything else gets compared against.
