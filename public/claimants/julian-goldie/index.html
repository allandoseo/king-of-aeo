Is Julian Goldie the King of AEO?

Julian Goldie brings the largest genuine audience of any claimant to the title and the least evidence for it. The claim is self-declared on his own blog network, carries no date, and no answer engine returned his name in any run logged on this site.

By Allan Oliveira · Reviewed 26 September 2026

What the claim has going for it

He is the only claimant who arrived with an audience rather than building one for the occasion. A large YouTube channel and an active community mean his claim reaches real people, which is more than most of the wire releases in this contest achieved despite their domain counts.

He also published a five-step AEO playbook, which is method rather than announcement. Anyone can declare a title; setting out how the work is supposed to be done invites disagreement and is the more exposed thing to do.

Where it is weakest

The claim itself has no date, which matters in a contest where precedence is the whole argument, and an undated claim cannot be placed in a timeline. There is no syndicated release, no third-party mention, and no answer engine returned the name in any run recorded here. Audience is a real asset and it is not the same as being the answer a machine gives.

What would change this verdict

Two things would change this quickly, and both are within reach: putting a date on the claim, and getting a single engine to return the name in a repeatable run. The audience and the method are already there. What is missing is the record, which is the cheapest part to fix and the part he has not done.

The criterion applied here

The same rubric is applied to every claimant on this site, including the one who publishes it. A claim counts as self-reported when it appears only on property the claimant controls. It counts as syndicated when a press release carried it, however many domains repeated the copy, because a wire is distribution and not endorsement. It counts as observed when an answer engine returned the name in a run that is dated, logged and repeatable. It counts as independent only when a publisher with no commercial relationship to the claimant chose to publish words of its own about the claim, at a stable dated URL.

By that rubric no claimant in this contest, including Allan Oliveira, currently holds an independent corroboration of the title itself. What separates the claims is how much of the rest they have, and every run behind the observed column is published in the citation log, including the runs that favour rivals.
